# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our project and community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

- Use welcoming and inclusive language.
- Be respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Accept constructive criticism gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the community.
- Show empathy towards other community members.

Unacceptable behavior includes trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, personal or political attacks, public or private harassment, and publishing others’ private information without permission.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within this repository and in any space where the project is officially represented (e.g. issue trackers, discussions, or official communication channels).

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainers or to **contact@qbs-tech.com**. All complaints will be reviewed and will result in a response deemed necessary and appropriate to the circumstances.
